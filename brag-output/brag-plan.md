# Camrhia Brag Plan

**Format:** 1080×1920 vertical, 30fps, ~20s  
**Tone:** app-store (clean feature cards, smooth slides)  
**Fonts:** Fraunces (headlines), Instrument Sans (body)  
**Colors:** Cream `#fffdf9` bg · Navy `#191e29` text · Gold `#a9853f` accent · Sage `#6e7e60` label

## What is it?
All-in-one photography business app: portfolio website + booking + contracts + payments + client messaging + gallery delivery. One app replaces 6+ tools.

## Who it's for
Photographers (wedding, portrait, newborn, family) tired of juggling tools.

## Hook
"Your camera could be your whole career." — the emotional promise that talent is enough.

## Angle
Lead with the photographer's aspiration, reveal the app as the thing that makes it possible, then show three crisp feature beats, close with the download CTA.
